Project Title : Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI Project

Short Description / Purpose:
The Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, and outlet trends using Blinkit sales data.
The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful visual insights, enabling better decision-making through KPI tracking, sales analysis, and outlet performance evaluation.
This project demonstrates the use of data analytics and business intelligence techniques to identify growth opportunities, improve operational understanding, and present data in a visually interactive manner.

Tech Stack:
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
Power BI – Dashboard creation & data visualization
Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – KPI calculations and measures
Microsoft Excel / CSV Dataset – Data source and preprocessing

Data Source:
Blinkit sales dataset containing:
Product details
Sales records
Outlet information
Customer ratings
Item visibility
Outlet establishment data

Features / Highlights:

Business Problem:
Retail businesses generate large volumes of sales and customer data, making it difficult to manually identify performance trends and business opportunities.
Blinkit required a centralized dashboard to:

Monitor overall sales performance
Analyze customer ratings and product demand
Compare outlet performance across locations and sizes
Track item visibility and category-wise sales
Support data-driven business decisions

Goal of the Dashboard:
The primary goal of this dashboard is to provide an interactive and easy-to-understand analytical view of Blinkit's sales data.
The dashboard helps:
Track key business KPIs
Identify high-performing products and outlets
Understand customer purchasing behavior
Compare sales across outlet types and locations
Improve strategic business decision-making

Walkthrough of Key Visuals:

1) KPI Cards
Displays important business metrics including:

Comparison of sales performance across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities.

7) Outlet Type Performance
Detailed table visualization showing:
Total Sales
Average Sales

8)nteractive Filters

Business Impact & Insights:
Key Insights Derived
Tier 3 outlets generated the highest sales contribution.
Medium-sized outlets showed better overall performance.
Fruits, snack foods, and household items were among the top-selling categories.
Regular fat products contributed more sales compared to low-fat products.
Supermarket Type 1 outlets had the highest sales and item count.

Business Impact:
Helps management identify profitable outlet categories.
Enables data-driven inventory and product planning.
Supports strategic expansion decisions based on outlet performance.
Improves visibility into customer preferences and sales behavior.
Provides a centralized performance monitoring solution.
